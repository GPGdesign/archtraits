About ARCHTRAITS

ARCHTRAITS is an architecture photography portfolio and digital magazine, capturing the beauty of architectural spaces through the essence of human experience.

At its essence, ARCHTRAITS is an attempt to merge architecture and portrait photography. The architectural environment becomes a frame for the person, and in turn, the person becomes the missing piece within the architecture, creating a sense of scale and humanization.

The images on this site represent my own photography portfolio.

Capturing the beauty of architecture
and the essence of human experience.

The ARCHTRAITS digital magazine features nine volumes, each pairing one photographer with one building: Alice Celentani at Palazzo della Civiltà Italiana, Giada Belleudi at Parco della Musica, Meli Ardila at FIU School of Architecture, Hera Sisi at Harpa Concert Hall, Kayli Fisher at Winter Park Library, Mackenzie Ryan at New World Center, Ana Mendez at the Innovation, Science, and Technology Building, Constanza Cescato at Museum Garage, and Molly Peyton at 1111 Lincoln Road.
